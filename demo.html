FlightSim147 — browser demo

The same procedural voxel engine as the ESP32 firmware, at the panel's 320×172 resolution and RGB565 colour.

Drag on the screen to steer: left/right to bank, up to climb, down to dive. Or use the arrow keys. Pick an aircraft and switch between the chase and cockpit views (keys 1–4 and V). Tilt to fly uses your phone's motion sensor, just like the 1.47B board. Hold the phone how you like, then tap it to set level. Let go and the autopilot takes over again.

Dogfight: shoot down each wave of red bandits. Guns fire automatically when a bandit is in your sights; hold GUNS (or Space) to fire anyway. Keep a bandit near the centre until the box turns red and says LOCK, then fire a MISSILE (M key). You carry 4, and they reload over time. Let go of the controls and the autopilot will chase the nearest bandit for you.
