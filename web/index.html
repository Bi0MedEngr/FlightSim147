FlightSim147

Procedural voxel flight sim for the Waveshare ESP32-S3 / ESP32-C6 1.47" LCD boards

▶ Try it in your browser first

This browser can't flash over USB. Use Chrome on Android (v148+) or desktop Chrome / Edge. Flashing needs a secure (https) page.
  1. Plug the board into your phone or computer with USB-C (use an OTG adapter on a phone if needed).
  2. Tap Install, pick the board's serial port, and confirm.
  3. If it won't connect: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again.
  4. When it's done, press RESET. Tap BOOT for a new world. On the 1.47B, hold BOOT for 1 s for tilt-to-fly.
  5. Later updates can go over Wi-Fi, including from an iPhone. Hold BOOT for 4 s and follow the screen. See the README.

The chip is detected automatically. ESP32-S3 and ESP32-C6 builds are both included. Source: github.com/Bi0MedEngr/FlightSim147
